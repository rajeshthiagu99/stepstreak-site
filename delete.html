Delete your StepStreak account

You can delete your account and all data linked to it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

Open StepStreak → Settings → Delete account. This deletes your account, your profile (first name), invite code, friends list and the daily step totals and streak stored for the friends leaderboard. It happens immediately.

Without the app

Email rajeshthiagu99@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject “Delete my StepStreak account”. We delete the same data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google as required by law; we keep no payment details. Step data in Apple Health or Health Connect stays on your phone under your control — StepStreak never writes to it.
